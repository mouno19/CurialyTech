How to use the system

01 · Color with intent

Let warm neutrals do most of the work. Use evergreen for progress and healthy states, coral for the key comparison or action, and amber for caution. Keep chart colors consistent with their legends.

02 · Type & hierarchy

Use Plus Jakarta Sans for short, confident headings and metric values. Use DM Sans for labels, descriptions, and controls. Lead each panel with a question or insight; keep supporting copy brief.

03 · Space & surfaces

Build with 15–16px gaps, generous 20–25px card padding, and soft 11–20px corners. Separate related information with space first; use quiet borders rather than heavy shadows.

Charts that explain

  • Give every chart a descriptive title, a unit, and a clear time period.
  • Use one accent for the primary series; mute historical or secondary data.
  • Show exact values in labels or tooltips, and never rely on color alone to communicate meaning.

States & accessibility

  • Pair positive, warning, and review colors with written status labels.
  • Keep body text readable against pale backgrounds; reserve light mint for accents on dark surfaces.
  • Make controls keyboard reachable with visible focus, and provide meaningful chart descriptions.

Design principle: Make the signal easy to spot, the context easy to understand, and the next step easy to choose.
